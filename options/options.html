Search your saved video titles and indexed transcripts. Your index lives only in this browser.

How would you like to start?

Import your past YouTube history, or start with an empty library. Importing is optional.

If you start empty, Recall builds your library from new videos you watch. You can import history later in settings.

Import from your YouTube account

Import your extracted watch-history.json from Google Takeout. Choose YouTube history in JSON format when exporting. Recall saves video titles, channels and watch dates locally and makes them searchable without fetching captions. The file is not uploaded.

Keep this page open while saving titles. Transcript fetching then continues in the background, even if you close settings. Reopen this page for progress or to pause after active videos finish. Use Fetch missing transcripts to retry unavailable captions or an interrupted job. Large histories can still take time. First use downloads the search model; thumbnails load from YouTube.

Find videos visited in this browser

Find YouTube videos you visited before installing Recall. History stays on this device. A visit does not necessarily mean you watched the video. This scans this browser's saved history, not your YouTube account history.

Or select up to 20 videos per batch. Transcript fetching runs in the background. YouTube may block captions; only successful videos become searchable by what was said.

Fetch transcripts

Paste YouTube links, one per line. Recall fetches captions in a separate minimized window. It closes after fetching finishes or pauses. Once fetching starts, settings can be closed. Keep the browser open and return here for progress.
