Library

What are moments?

A moment is a searchable piece of a video or page: spoken words, on-screen text or a passage you read. Several moments can come from one item. Video matches link to a timestamp; page matches highlight the saved passage when supported. Scenes are saved images and are counted separately.

What gets saved?

Public page text, up to three visible article images and compatible video evidence on allowed sites. Incognito, known private services, private routes, visible sign-out controls and password/editor pages are excluded. These checks cannot recognize every private page. New text redacts recognizable personal identifiers; images are not redacted. Past page bodies are not fetched.

Keep your saved moments safe

Your library lives only in this browser, with no account or cloud sync. Export a backup before uninstalling, clearing this browser profile or moving to another computer, then restore it there to search everything again.

What's in a backup?

Included: everything that makes your saved moments searchable. That's each video's title, channel, link, watch date and full transcript with timestamps; each page's title, link and saved text; scene snapshots and article images; and on-screen text read from videos.

Not included: original video files, audio recordings or complete websites, only what CueWoven saved from them. Settings, site permissions and AI models aren't included either; set them again after restoring, and the models download again. If a video or page is later removed online, your saved moments from it stay searchable, but the backup can't bring the original back.

Restoring: CueWoven rebuilds its search data on this computer, so a large library with many images can take a while and keep your computer busy. Keep this page open until it finishes. If it's interrupted, the parts already restored stay.

Privacy: the backup file contains your transcripts, page text and images. Keep it private and don't share it.
