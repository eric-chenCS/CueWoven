Library

What are moments?

A moment is a searchable piece of a video or page: spoken words, on-screen text or a passage you read. Several moments can come from one item. Video matches link to a timestamp; page matches highlight the saved passage when supported. Scenes are saved images and are counted separately.

    What gets saved?

    Only public page text and compatible video evidence on allowed sites. Incognito, known private services, private routes, visible sign-out controls and password/editor pages are excluded. These checks cannot recognize every private page. New text redacts recognizable personal identifiers; images are not redacted. Past page bodies are not fetched.
