Search videos, pages, articles and past queries. Your index lives only in this browser.

How would you like to start?

Import your past video, browser or search history, or start with an empty library. Importing is optional.

Start with YouTube, or enable optional website capture below. You can import history later.

Import websites and Google searches

Choose extracted Google Takeout Chrome BrowserHistory.json or Google Search My Activity JSON. Titles, addresses, queries and dates become searchable locally. This does not download old pages, videos or private account content. Sensitive addresses are skipped. Use the YouTube importer below for YouTube history.

Websites & articles

Optional: save text from articles and pages you read, plus available captions, sampled scenes and on-screen text from visible playing videos on websites such as Instagram and TikTok. This works in the browser, not native apps. Some protected or cross-origin players cannot expose images, captions or audio. Only grant sites you trust Recall to remember.

Allow public websites requests access to HTTP/HTTPS sites. Email, known financial/health/government sites, private paths, password/editor pages and incognito are excluded. These checks cannot recognize every private or signed-in page; use specific sites for tighter control. Form values are never read. No past pages are fetched.

Text and media stay in this browser. First use downloads local model weights. Text capture keeps up to 2,000 words per page; full page/video-text storage is limited to 2,000 items or 64 MiB, then older items keep their titles. Scene/OCR limits below also apply across video sites.

Import from your YouTube account

Import your extracted watch-history.json from Google Takeout. Choose YouTube history in JSON format when exporting. Recall saves video titles, channels and watch dates locally and makes them searchable without fetching captions. The file is not uploaded.

Keep this page open while saving titles. Transcript fetching then continues in the background, even if you close settings. Reopen this page for progress or to pause after active videos finish. Use Fetch missing transcripts to retry unavailable captions or an interrupted job. Large histories can still take time. First use downloads the search model; thumbnails load from YouTube.

Find videos visited in this browser

Find YouTube videos you visited before installing Recall. History stays on this device. A visit does not necessarily mean you watched the video. This scans this browser's saved history, not your YouTube account history.

Or select up to 20 videos per batch. Transcript fetching runs in the background. YouTube may block captions; only successful videos become searchable by what was said.

Fetch transcripts

Paste YouTube links, one per line. Recall fetches captions in a separate minimized window. It closes after fetching finishes or pauses. Once fetching starts, settings can be closed. Keep the browser open and return here for progress.
