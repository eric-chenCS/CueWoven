# CueWoven v3.3.28

**One results layout.** The best match is a larger card with its thumbnail beside the text, short enough that alternatives stay in view. **Other matches** below it holds up to three more videos or pages as compact rows (thumbnail and timestamp, title, channel and watch date), one row per video, and **Show more** reveals more rows, loading further distinct videos 12 at a time. The **Best match only** checkbox, the ← → arrows and the separate near-tie hint are removed. ↑ and ↓ move through every result, and Enter opens the selected one or the best match.

**Confident by default.** Other matches start folded when the best match is confident, so a clear answer reads as one. They open by themselves when the engine is unsure: when the top two nearly tie (the second row says **Almost as good**) or when the best match is only related in meaning.

**Settings.** "Moments per video" is removed, since rows are now one per video. Reset settings no longer writes the unused best-match-only preference. The popup requests distinct videos with paging (`distinct: true`, 12 per page); the older `singleVideo` and list request shapes still work for the address-bar search and scripts.

Validation: all 287 Node tests pass, including the distinct-video request shape and its position checks. In Chromium, the single-video, Enter-search, demo-fixes, icons-and-matches, refine, minimal UI, v3.3 features, gray theme and preferences browser checks pass. The rewritten single-video check compares the popup with the real model and ranker across two pages of 14 distinct videos, filters, details and a stale Show more reply. A separate check confirmed folded and auto-opened states, hiding the best match, and light/dark layouts without horizontal overflow. The scenes check fails at its image-search step on v3.3.27 as well in this environment, and search-accuracy needs a local model folder, so neither ran. No permissions, dependencies or storage format changed.
